## AI and Robotics (Assessment Week, Units 3 and 4)
### Aston University - Foundations of AI and Data Science (2025-26)

## Lecture Overview

Week 11 is the assessment week for Units 3 and 4 (search, CSPs, nature-inspired algorithms and neural networks). Instead of a standard lecture, the week featured two guest talks from Aston researchers on applied AI and robotics:

- **Applications of Applied AI and Robotics at Small Scale**, Dr Ferhat Sadak (Assistant Professor in Applied AI and Robotics, School of Computer Science and Digital Technologies)
- **Industrial Automation for Healthcare: Challenges and Opportunities**, Dr Amir Hajiyavand

These were guest presentations of the speakers' own current research rather than core, testable module content, so this note stays brief and does not reproduce their specific published findings or citations.

## What I Took From It

- AI and robotics research at Aston spans a wide range of physical scales, from microrobotics (manipulating objects at the microscale, e.g. in biological or microfluidic contexts) up to industrial-scale automation
- Deep learning is increasingly used as the perception and control layer for robotics, not just for image classification, examples included tracking and pose estimation for small robotic systems
- Industrial automation in healthcare settings brings extra constraints beyond a typical factory: safety, regulation and reliability requirements are much stricter when the system interacts with clinical environments or patient care
- Both talks reinforced a theme running through the whole module: the search, optimisation and learning techniques from Units 3 and 4 are not just academic exercises, they underpin real deployed systems

## Connection to the Rest of the Module

- The nature-inspired algorithms from Week 8 (ACO, PSO, evolutionary algorithms) are directly relevant to robot motion planning and multi-robot coordination
- The neural network concepts from Week 10 (CNNs in particular) are the basis of the perception systems mentioned in both talks
- This is a good example of the module's own framing: search and learning are foundational techniques that show up again once you look at a real applied AI system

## Key Terms for Week 11

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Applied AI | AI research aimed at solving a specific real-world problem rather than advancing general theory |
| Microrobotics | Robotics operating at very small physical scales, often in biological or microfluidic contexts |
| Industrial automation | Using AI and robotics to automate physical processes in an industrial or clinical setting |
